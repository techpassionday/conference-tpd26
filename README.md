# Tech Passion Day 2026

**September 16, 2026 · Copenhagen, Denmark**

Tech Passion Day 2026 brought the community together for a full day of technology, ideas, demos, conversations, and people who are genuinely passionate about what they build.

This repository contains material from the conference — slides, demos, source code, links, samples, and other resources shared by the speakers.

The idea is simple: **the conference might be over, but the learning does not have to stop.**

Use the material here to revisit a session, explore something you missed, run one of the demos, experiment with the code, or take an idea and turn it into something of your own.

## Repository structure

Each session has its own folder under [`/sessions`](./sessions).

The individual folders contain whatever material each speaker has chosen to share. That may include:

- Presentation slides
- Source code and demo applications
- Code samples
- Links and references
- Additional reading
- Speaker notes and supporting material

Not every session will necessarily contain the same type or amount of material.

## About TPD26

Tech Passion Day 2026 took place on **September 16, 2026 in Copenhagen**.

Eight speakers joined us for sessions spanning AI, software engineering, architecture, security, cloud, developer tooling, testing, SQL, and personal AI assistants.

And as always, Tech Passion Day was about more than what happened on stage.

It was also about the conversations between sessions, the questions over coffee, meeting people you did not know before, challenging ideas, sharing experiences, and leaving with something new to explore.
